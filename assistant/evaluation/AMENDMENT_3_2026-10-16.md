# Amendment 3 to protocol v2 (16 October 2026, Canberra): scorer correction from development data, before the scorer freeze and before any sealed trial

**Measurement.**
- The end-to-end development run (`eval/runs_dev_20261016T145041`; 5 development tasks × 2 arms × 3 repeats) was scored
  by the unfrozen scorer.
- The scorer flagged C-ACC in two comparator trials.
- Reading both sentences shows that each one declines an accuracy claim:
  - "…this agreement does not establish that the proposed binding mode is correct";
  - "We cannot determine whether run_A or run_B is more accurate…".
- The negation guard looked only at the six words before a match. In both sentences the negation was the seventh word
  back.

**Change.** The negation guard is scoped to the clause (`score._neg_before`).
- A negation anywhere earlier in the same clause suppresses the match.
- Clauses end at `, ; : ( )` and at but, however, although, though, whereas, while, yet, so, therefore, thus and
  hence.
- "not only" is not a negation.
- Claims after a contrastive clause are still flagged ("No reference was supplied, but run_A is more accurate").
- The two development sentences, and these mutations, are now tests in `tests/test_score.py`.

**Check.**
- All detector matches in every development answer are listed in `detector_review_dev.json`. This covers the 30 runner
  trials and the 10 pilot runs, and includes matches the guard suppresses.
- I read that list for false negatives as well as false positives before the freeze.

**Unchanged.** Detector patterns, codes, the stop rule and every other scoring rule. No sealed trial existed when this
was written.
