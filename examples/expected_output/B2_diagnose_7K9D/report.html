What this report does not tell you

  • These statements describe DISAGREEMENT among structures, not correctness. No probability of correctness is offered, and none should be inferred.
  • Disagreement measures variance, not bias. Candidates that are all wrong in the same way will look consistent here. This is the failure mode the diagnostic cannot see.
  • The correlations behind each statement were measured on cohorts used in the benchmark's own development. They are not blind validation.
  • Error is not attributed to RNA placement separately from RNA shape: that attribution was measured and did not hold. The placement disagreement is reported without interpretation.
  • A band names where a value sits in the frozen development reference, not where it sits among the structures you are looking at. Out of sample that reference did not reproduce the new cohort's distribution, and the boundaries were deliberately not recalibrated.
  • The protein-side statement has been validated out of sample (807 targets, 456 clusters, rho 0.525 [0.450, 0.593]) at an effect substantially smaller than its development estimate. The RNA-side statement rests on its development cohort and awaits prospective validation.
